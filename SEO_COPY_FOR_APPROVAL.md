### 5.2 "I believe" claims (approve 3–5)

#### 5.2A
**AI literacy is a workplace skill, not a technical one.** The people who most need it are the working professionals who weren't invited to the AI conversation in the first place — and they're the ones being asked to use it tomorrow.

#### 5.2B
**Conversational AI for banks fails when it tries to have a personality.** People don't want a friend at their bank. They want a working bank. Competence builds trust faster than charm does.

#### 5.2C
**Content design is a product discipline, not a writing discipline.** A great content designer is the one product partners ask for by name — because the work makes the product clearer, faster, and safer to ship.

#### 5.2D
**The best AI products in regulated industries were built by people who could be told "no" five different ways and still ship something good.** Legal, compliance, accessibility, and brand are not blockers. They're the design.

#### 5.2E
**Small businesses deserve the same craft that big brands buy.** Most of them are paying for templates and calling it strategy. Trustfall exists because that gap shouldn't be this wide.

#### 5.2F
**Most AI literacy training fails because it teaches the tool, not the judgment.** The skill working professionals actually need is knowing when to trust the output, when to push back, and when to put the model down.
